Our Story

Crafted in Small Batches, Worn for a Lifetime

Wristhaus began in 2019 with a single question: why does jewelry that's meant to last so rarely does? We set out to design bracelets, watches and other wrist accessories that hold up to real life — waterproof, tarnish-free, and made with the kind of care that shows.

Handcrafted, Not Mass-Produced

Every Piece Passes Through Human Hands

Our artisans work in small studios, not factories. Each bracelet is measured, set, and finished individually — which means slight variations, and also means someone actually cared about the one you're wearing.

Built to Actually Wear

Waterproof, Hypoallergenic, Tarnish-Free

Shower in it. Swim in it. Forget you're wearing it. Every finish is engineered to resist tarnish and skin sensitivity, so your bracelet Combo still looks new a year from now — not just on day one.
