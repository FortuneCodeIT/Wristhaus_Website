Frequently Asked

Quick Answers

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days and arrive in 3–5 days domestically. Free shipping on orders over $50.

Can I return or exchange a bracelet?

Yes — unworn items in original packaging can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery.

Are your bracelets waterproof?

All current collections are waterproof and tarnish-resistant, safe for showering, swimming, and everyday wear.

Do you offer custom engraving?

Select personalized pieces can be engraved at checkout. Look for the "Personalize" option on the product page.
